# Visual Studio 2.1: Exploring Medium Through Prompt Variation

## Prompt A – Watercolor Painting

Create a watercolor painting of a Nepali street food vendor at night in Basantapur, Kathmandu, with the old pagoda temples of Durbar Square softly visible in the background. The vendor is steaming momos and tossing chowmein in a wok, with hot potatoes and chicken BBQ skewers on the side of his cart. Emphasize contrast between the warm glowing light of the cart and the deep blue night, color in soft orange and golden washes, texture in the rising steam and BBQ smoke, and emphasis on the vendor's face lit by the fire. Use round brushes, cold-press watercolor paper, and transparent pigments. Use the wet-on-wet technique so the light bleeds and glows into the night sky, and layered glazes to build the shadows, with soft bleeding edges where the colors meet.

## Prompt B – Linocut Print

Create a linocut print of a Nepali street food vendor at night in Basantapur, Kathmandu, with the old pagoda temples of Durbar Square visible in the background. The vendor is steaming momos and tossing chowmein in a wok, with hot potatoes and chicken BBQ skewers on the side of his cart. Emphasize contrast between the bright white light of the cart and the solid black night, line in bold carved strokes for the rising steam and BBQ smoke, texture in carved patterns on the temple roofs, cart, and stone street, and emphasis on the vendor's face lit by the fire. Use a linoleum block carved with V-gouges and U-gouges, a brayer to roll black ink, and press it onto paper. Keep high-contrast shapes, crisp carved edges, and visible gouge marks.
