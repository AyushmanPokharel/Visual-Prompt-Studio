# Still Here

## Genre
Acoustic Folk / Americana

## Prompt
Create an album cover for an acoustic folk band. A man sits on the shore of a calm lake, leaning against a large tree while he plays his guitar and sings a hopeful, motivating song about life. The scene takes place at sunset. The brightest light comes from the setting sun low on the horizon, which reflects across the water, while the man and the tree are darker shapes in the foreground, surrounded by soft shadows. To create depth, the man and the tree are large and close to the viewer, the lake stretches out in the middle, and the sun and a pale rising moon sit small and far away in the background, fading into a hazy sky. The sun setting while the moon rises shows time passing from day to night, and a flock of birds flying across the sky and wind rippling the water add a sense of gentle movement. Warm orange and gold sunset colors repeat across the sky, water, and wood of the tree, which unifies the whole cover, while a single red flower growing beside the tree adds variety. The layout is asymmetrical: the big tree and the man fill the left side, which makes them feel more important than the small sun and moon in the distance.

## Vocabulary Breakdown
- **Value:** Value is how light or dark something is, like the bright sun compared to the dark side of the tree.
- **Space:** Space is how a flat picture looks deep, like the big tree and the man that are close to us, while the hills, the setting sun, the clouds, and the birds look small and far away.
- **Unity:** Unity is when everything looks like it belongs to each other, like the light yellowish orange color that shows up in the sky, the water, and the tree.
- **Balance:** Balance is how the weight is spread out in a picture, like the big, dark tree and the man on the left being balanced by the bright sun on the right.
- **Rhythm:** Rhythm is when something repeats and moves your eye along, like the birds flying in a line and the lake water waving.
