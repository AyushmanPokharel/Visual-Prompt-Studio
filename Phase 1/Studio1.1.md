# Orbital Market

## Prompt
Create a promotional poster for a science fiction movie set in a crowded futuristic night market. The market is packed with stalls and people, but it is clean and well-maintained. Tall, straight metal poles create sharp vertical lines that lead the viewer's eye up toward a giant robot in the center of the market. Curved organic lines from cables hanging between the poles sag across the scene and frame the robot. Round lanterns hang from the cables and repeat a circular shape throughout the poster, which contrasts with the rectangular shapes of the poles and stalls. The crowd fills the edges of the poster, but there is empty negative space around the robot to make it feel isolated and threatening. The robot has a sleek, shiny metal texture, and it feels heavy and massive, towering over the small people below. The robot glows red against the dark street and shadows, creating strong contrast so it becomes the focal point and looks both beautiful and scary.

## Vocabulary Breakdown
- **Line:** A line is a mark that goes from one point to another, and it can be curvy, wavy, or in different forms, just like the cables we see there, or straight like the poles.
- **Shape:** A shape is flat, like the moon behind the robot's head, which looks like a flat circle.
- **Texture:** Texture is how something feels when you touch it, or how it looks like it would feel, like the robot, which looks hard and shiny.
- **Volume:** Volume is when something looks 3D, so you can see its other sides and angles, like the banners and the giant robot.
- **Contrast:** Contrast is when bright and dark things are next to each other, so something stands out, like the glowing robot against the gloomy dark sky.
